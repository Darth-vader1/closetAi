1. 3D Layered Lookbook Stage (Interactive Three.js / React Three Fiber)
What it is: When the AI Stylist or Studio suggests an outfit, instead of just flat side-by-side squares, the clothes are presented on an interactive 3D styling stage:
The top, jacket, trousers, shoes, and accessories float in realistic 3D depth with subtle breathing/floating physics.
You can orbit around the look in 3D with your mouse or finger, rotate the camera, and see how the layers stack (e.g., jacket over shirt, trousers under, shoes grounded with real-time soft shadows).
Tech: Lightweight @react-three/fiber + three.js. Fast to load, runs smoothly on mobile browsers and desktops.
2. 3D Mannequin / Virtual Try-On Silhouette
What it is: A sleek, stylized 3D mannequin model (like a high-fashion atelier dummy or glass/ceramic figure):
The selected outfit is mapped onto the mannequin in 3D space (torso for tops, legs for bottoms, feet for shoes).
You can spin the mannequin 360°, inspect the silhouette from the side or back, and toggle layers (e.g., take the jacket off to see the shirt beneath).
3. 3D Virtual Walk-in Closet / Showroom
What it is: A minimalist virtual boutique or walk-in closet in 3D:
Clothes are arranged on racks (clean clothes on the main rail, laundry basket in the corner).
You can pan across the rails, tap a hanger to inspect the garment, or drag items together to test an outfit combination.
4. AI 2D-to-3D Garment Reconstruction (Tripo3D / Meshy API)
What it is: When a user uploads a photo of a shoe, bag, or jacket, an AI 3D model generator converts the single 2D photo into a true 3D .glb mesh!
The user can literally inspect their favorite sneaker or leather jacket as a textured 3D asset.
